1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the Sri Sri Radha Krishna Chandra – Admin Command Center mobile application, the connected administration website, and associated administrative modules used for temple operations. The application is intended for authorized administrators, staff, and volunteers rather than for unrestricted public use.

The service is provided for the administration of Sri Sri Radha Krishna Chandra Mandir / ISKCON Hadapsar, Pune, India. It supports account authentication, staff access controls, device registration, temple content management, activity recording, and duty-location monitoring.

Important privacy disclosureThe application can collect precise location while an authorized duty-tracking service is active, including in the background or when the app is not in use. The location service is designed to show a persistent Android notification. Read Section 3 before using this feature.

The administrator-facing portal contains links to several separate temple modules. When a module connects to a different database, cloud platform, or external service, the information supplied to that module may also be processed by that provider.

3. Location tracking, including background use

When tracking begins

In the reviewed implementation, the portal requests the native tracking service after a user signs in or when an authenticated session is restored. The Android foreground service can continue running when the app is minimized or its activity is no longer visible. The implementation also attempts to restart the service after the app task is removed, subject to Android's restrictions.

What is collected and how frequently

The native service requests GPS or network-provider updates with an approximately eight-second minimum interval and has an approximately 12-second heartbeat to report a last-known position, if available. Actual timing, accuracy and delivery depend on Android, provider availability, power restrictions and connectivity. The stored location can be a last-known reading rather than a fresh GPS fix. For each submitted record, the app may include coordinates, accuracy, movement speed, provider, battery information, device model, Android ID and timestamps.

How it is used

Duty tracking is intended for authorized temple operations, duty coordination and safety-related status checks. The implementation stores a current live-location record and a dated movement-history entry in a Firebase Realtime Database. It can also record when GPS is switched off. Authorized administrative personnel may use these records to review duty activity and operational alerts.

Stopping location collection

Location permission can be revoked in Android Settings, under Apps → this app → Permissions → Location. You may also disable device location services or force-stop the app from Android Settings. Important: in the version of code reviewed for this policy, signing out of the web dashboard does not itself reliably stop the native duty-tracking service. Therefore, do not assume that web sign-out has ended background location collection. Contact the administrator if you need the associated historical data removed.

Location is not described as being used for advertising or the sale of location data in the reviewed application code.

4. Purposes for processing data

  • Account access: sign-in, identity checks, administration permissions, and account/device restrictions.
  • Fraud prevention and security: detecting duplicate device registration, suspicious account access, and blocked devices or accounts.
  • Duty coordination: monitoring current duty location, historical movement and GPS-status alerts for authorized operational use.
  • Temple administration: managing enrolments, programmes, content, live-stream settings, images, announcements, and other administrative records.
  • Technical operation: remembering user preferences, reconnecting to the portal, file selection and displaying notifications.
  • Support and accountability: reviewing access and activity records when investigating a support, security or operational issue.

Access to tracking should be limited to staff members for whom it is necessary and explained prior to permission requests. The availability of a feature in source code does not replace the need for proper authorization and informed notice.

6. Cloud providers and sharing

Administrative information is sent to cloud services used to operate the app. Depending on the specific feature accessed, these may include:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Firestore, and Cloud Messaging for identities, admin records, location updates, and message operations.
  • Vercel: hosting the administration website and server endpoints; standard request metadata such as IP address may be processed to deliver the site.
  • GitHub: for administrators who intentionally use the cloud image/content-management integration; associated API calls and credentials may be processed by GitHub.
  • Image upload/hosting services: selected images may be sent to an image-storage service (including ImgBB in a reviewed module) when an administrator uploads content.
  • Third-party content services: selected modules may connect to YouTube or live-stream/video technology providers to deliver relevant functionality.

We do not describe location information as being sold to data brokers or used for targeted advertising. Cloud providers may process data in different regions under their own terms and technical requirements. Administrators may also intentionally publish selected temple content, such as announcements or images, for public viewing.

7. Security, storage and access

The reviewed web portal uses Firebase Authentication for administrator sign-in and HTTPS URLs to communicate with the hosting and cloud services. Device-registration and access-restriction checks are included in the client implementation. Android's permission system limits device-location access.

Security also depends on configured Firebase database rules, cloud credentials, administrative access controls and the published Android build. Client-side checks alone are not sufficient authorization. No internet-connected system can guarantee complete security; if you believe information has been accessed improperly, please contact us promptly.

We recommend that administrators use strong unique passwords, avoid credential sharing, protect devices with screen locks, and avoid saving sensitive GitHub access tokens on shared devices. Information entered into external administration modules may be subject to those modules' storage arrangements.

8. Data retention and deletion

Account and device-binding information may be retained while an administrator account is active or while needed for access-control and security purposes. Duty-location history and audit data are stored in cloud databases for operational review.

Current implementation note: the reviewed code creates date-grouped movement history but does not implement an automatic 24-hour deletion rule or a verified fixed retention period. Unless an authorized administrator deletes the records or server-side rules outside the reviewed code remove them, they may remain stored. Do not interpret the phrase “24-hour movement trail” in source comments as a promise that history is erased after 24 hours.

Local preferences and cached information can generally be cleared from Android application storage. Cloud-stored location, audit and identity records require an authorized deletion process. You may request access, correction or deletion using the contact route in Section 13. Requests will be reviewed after appropriate identity and account verification; some records may need to be retained where required by applicable law or for a documented legitimate security reason.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

  • You can review or change granted app permissions through Android Settings.
  • You can decline optional media access and choose individual files for upload where supported.
  • You can ask the administrator why access to a location or account-related permission is required for your duties.
  • You can request a copy, correction or deletion of information associated with your account by contacting the operator below and providing enough information to verify the request.
  • Removing the app or signing out does not automatically delete data previously stored in Firebase or other cloud services.

Access to this staff administration system may be restricted if a permission is essential to a particular authorized duty feature. Any restriction should be explained by the responsible administrator.

10. Children's information and programme records

The Admin Command Center is intended for authorized adults and is not designed for children to log in independently. Some temple modules manage programmes attended by children, including admissions and follow-ups. Such records can contain a child's name, age, guardian name, telephone number, area and administrative notes.

Staff should enter only information necessary for the programme, use appropriate parent or guardian authorization where required, restrict access, and avoid placing children's private details in publicly accessible content. This app's duty-location tracking is intended for authorized administrators, not for tracking children through this admin application.
